Five Lives is a health-technology company building evidence-based digital tools for dementia prevention and brain health for adults aged 50 and over. Founded by former Peak brain-training leaders Xavier Louis (CEO) and Sylvain Piquet (COO), the fully remote company operates two consumer mobile apps: Five Lives Brain Health, a prevention app available since 2021 with cognitive tests, brain games, and lifestyle tracking across five pillars (nutrition, sleep, physical activity, mood and social connection, and mental stimulation); and Five Lives Care, an evidence-based memory and autonomy programme for people aged 60+ launched in January 2026 and developed with leading memory centres in the UK and France. Its work draws on the FINGER study and the Lancet Commission's modifiable dementia risk factors, with research partners including the University of Oxford, Trinity College Dublin, the FINGER Brain Health Institute, Dementias Platform UK, and the Gerontopole d'Ile de France. Backed by Speedinvest. Five Lives does not currently publish a public developer API, SDK, or developer portal.

## How this profile is made
